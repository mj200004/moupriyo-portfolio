PROJECT_04 Urban Street Flower Classification
Python  ·  TensorFlow  ·  Keras  ·  InceptionV3  ·  ResNet50  ·  NumPy  ·  Matplotlib
A deep learning model for classifying 17 species of urban street flowers and trees, fine-tuned from a pretrained InceptionV3 network on the Kaggle Urban Street Classification dataset. Employs fine-tuning on deep feature layers layered with a custom head block (GlobalAveragePooling2D → Dense 4096 ReLU → BatchNormalization → Dense 17 Softmax) with dynamic learning-rate step downs. Achieved a final validation accuracy of 96.05%.
