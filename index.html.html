PROJECT_02 AQI Prediction using RFM & Clustering
Python  ·  Pandas  ·  NumPy  ·  Scikit-learn  ·  Matplotlib  ·  K-Means  ·  kneed  ·  Jupyter
An Air Quality Index (AQI) prediction and segmentation system built on Pune air quality data (SO₂, NOx, RSPM, SPM) sourced from the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB). The pipeline applies RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) feature engineering per location, uses the Elbow Method with KneeLocator to determine the optimal cluster count, and trains a K-Means model for unsupervised AQI segmentation across 6,219 samples. Clusters are validated against median AQI-based ground truth labels. The codebase is fully modular with dedicated scripts for preprocessing, RFM analysis, clustering, visualization, and configuration.

PROJECT_03 Urban Street Flower Classification
Python  ·  TensorFlow  ·  Keras  ·  InceptionV3  ·  ResNet50  ·  NumPy  ·  Matplotlib
A deep learning model for classifying 17 species of urban street flowers and trees, fine-tuned from a pretrained InceptionV3 network (ImageNet weights) on the Kaggle Urban Street Classification dataset — 1,828 training, 228 validation, and 219 test images. Layers 0–248 are frozen while layers 249 onwards are fine-tuned. A custom head (GlobalAveragePooling2D → Dense 4096 ReLU → BatchNormalization → Dense 17 Softmax) is appended. Training uses the Adam optimizer with categorical cross-entropy loss, ReduceLROnPlateau scheduling (factor=√0.1, patience=5), and full data augmentation (rotation, shift, shear, zoom, flip). Achieved a final validation accuracy of 96.05%, with a peak of 98.25% at epoch 22 across 50 epochs.
